Privacy Policy

Qisas wa Maqulat Publishing Pipeline · Last updated: 20 October 2026

The Qisas wa Maqulat Publishing Pipeline (“the Application”) is a private software tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel قصص ومقولات (@qisaswamaqulat) to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube features through the Application, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and this Application’s use of data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

What the Application does

  • Uploads and schedules videos to the owner’s own channel, and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and caption tracks.
  • Adds the owner’s videos to the owner’s own playlists.
  • Reads analytics for the owner’s own channel to produce a weekly performance report for the owner.
  • Reads public comments posted on the owner’s own videos and posts replies to them as the channel.

The Application only ever accesses the single YouTube channel of its owner. It never accesses other users’ accounts or channels and never posts outside the owner’s own videos.

Information accessed

  • The owner’s account data, through Google OAuth: channel details, videos, playlists, captions and YouTube Analytics reports for the owner’s channel.
  • Viewer comments on the owner’s videos: the comment text, the commenter’s public display name and public channel ID, as provided by the YouTube API. These are used only to decide whether and how to reply to the comment.

The Application does not collect contact details, does not use cookies or tracking, and does not collect any information from people who are not the channel owner beyond the public comment data described above.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the owner’s channel: publishing the owner’s videos, organising them into playlists, replying to comments, and producing performance reports for the owner. Comment text and video titles may be sent to an AI language model provider (Anthropic) solely to draft replies and reports; the Application does not use this data for any other purpose.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens and the data the Application works with are stored only on the owner’s own computer. Comment data is kept only as a record of which comments have been answered, and analytics data only as part of the owner’s private reports. Stored YouTube data is refreshed or deleted in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies, and in any case within 30 days unless still needed to provide the functions above.

Sharing

The Application does not sell, rent or share YouTube data with any third party, other than the AI provider used to draft replies and reports as described above. No data is used for advertising.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the Application’s access to their Google account at any time from the Google security settings page, which stops all access immediately. Viewers who no longer want their comments considered for replies can delete their comments on YouTube at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/khalidnaez/qisas-pipeline-privacy/issues or through the contact options on the channel’s About page.
