Qisas wa Maqulat Publishing Pipeline

A private publishing tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel قصص ومقولات (@qisaswamaqulat) to manage that one channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's videos as private and schedules their publication time.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, custom thumbnails and caption tracks on the owner's videos.
  • Organises the owner's videos into the owner's own playlists.
  • Replies to viewer comments on the owner's own videos, as the channel.
  • Produces a weekly analytics report for the owner from YouTube Analytics.

Access

The owner authorises the tool with Google OAuth 2.0. It acts only on the owner's own channel and never on any other account or channel. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.
